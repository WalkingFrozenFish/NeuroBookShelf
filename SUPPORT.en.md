English and Russian are welcome. Include the application version, OS and architecture, installation type and model status. For PDF problems, state whether the document contains text or is a scan, give the page number and reproduction steps.

Do not publish private documents or your full library database. A small anonymous sample is more useful.

The project is maintained as time permits. Paid support and guaranteed response times are not offered.
